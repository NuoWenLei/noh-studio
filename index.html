No One's Headspace Studio

A private studio for making No One's Headspace, a YouTube channel of short, data-driven videos.

Uses YouTube API Services
Uploads the owner's own videos to their own YouTube channel, as private videos.

By using the studio you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Your data is handled under our Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What the studio does

  • Builds each finished episode into a YouTube package: the video, captions, a cover image and a description listing every data source.
  • Uploads that package to the owner's channel through the YouTube Data API, always as a private video.
  • The owner reviews the video and publishes it in YouTube Studio.

The studio is used only by its owner, Nuo Wen Lei. It is not offered to other people.
